Which material should I choose?＋

Start with the material explorer and your intended use. Embroidered, woven, PVC, chenille and leather each create a different surface. The final choice should be checked against your artwork and finished dimensions.

Can a small logo or fine lettering work?＋

Share the artwork at the size you intend to use. Readability depends on material, line weight, lettering and construction. Highlight any detail that must stay intact.

What dimensions and quantity should I provide?＋

Include your intended width and height, the units you are using and the number of patches you are considering. Exact minimums and pricing must be confirmed in the quote; the brief does not establish a minimum order.

What backing options are available?＋

Tell us how the patch will be used and whether you prefer sew-on, heat-applied, hook-and-loop or another attachment. These are preferences to discuss; availability and suitability must be confirmed for the material and item.

Can I share a deadline?＋

Yes. Include a required date and delivery destination in your brief. Production and delivery timing must be confirmed before an order is placed. This preview does not promise a turnaround time.

What happens before production?＋

The artwork, dimensions, material, attachment and commercial terms should be reviewed and approved. Proof, sample and revision arrangements must be agreed for your specific project.

Where are the patches made and shipped?＋

PATCHSMITH patches are made in Pakistan and shipped worldwide. Destination-specific shipping arrangements, charges and any import requirements must be confirmed in the quote.

Does the project form send a request?＋

No. It builds a local, reviewable brief in your browser. You can download the text, but nothing is sent to PATCHSMITH. There are no payments, uploads or orders in this preview.

Are these real customer samples?＋

No. All patch artwork shown here is original conceptual illustration for the website design. It is not evidence of a finished production sample, customer project or specific manufacturing outcome.
