03 / Know what’s next

A clear path.
A considered patch.

From first idea to the final detail. Here’s what each conversation needs to resolve.

SUNSET / STITCHED IDENTITY CONCEPT
01The idea＋

Share the artwork and context.

Tell us what the patch is for, the size you have in mind and your preferred material. Unknowns can stay open in your initial brief.

02The specification＋

Turn the idea into a clear plan.

Review material, dimensions, color, edge and backing. Confirm pricing, quantities and timing in the quote before making a commitment.

03The approval＋

Know what you are approving.

Review the agreed artwork and specification before production. Any proof, sample and revision arrangements must be confirmed for your project.

04The production＋

Make it. Check it. Send it.

Production and delivery follow the approved project terms. The schedule, destination and shipping arrangements should be agreed before the order is placed.
